# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive in issues, reviews and discussions. Welcome questions, explain disagreements and focus criticism on the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, spam and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation to protect contributors.

For a conduct concern, ask a maintainer for a private contact route without posting identifying or sensitive details in public. Do not publish personal information as part of a report. No dedicated confidential reporting service is currently provided.
